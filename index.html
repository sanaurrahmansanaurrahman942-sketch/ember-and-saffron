Our Story

Where fire meets patience

Ember & Saffron was born from a simple belief — that the best food cannot be rushed. Our clay tandoor, fired with seasoned wood, has been the heart of our kitchen for generations. Every naan, every kebab, every slow-simmered nihari carries the warmth of that ancient flame.

We source our spices from the bazaars of Peshawar, our meat from trusted local farms, and our recipes from the hands of grandmothers who never wrote them down. Here, cooking is not a science — it is a memory, passed down through smoke and saffron.
